# Incident Findings — Submission Template

Fill in every section below. Where a section asks for evidence, link to or
paste the actual logs, metrics, or command output you used — assertions
without evidence will not be credited.

## Incident Impact

What was affected? How large was the impact?

## Timeline

Important timestamps and events.

## Hypotheses

What did you consider? What evidence supported or rejected each one?

## Root Cause

Exact technical mechanism.

## Causal Evidence

Proof that the proposed root cause caused the observed behaviour.

## Reproduction

A script, test, or set of instructions that reliably trigger the problem.

## Immediate Mitigation

How would you safely reduce customer impact during the incident?

## Permanent Fix

The code or design change you made.

## Validation

Evidence that the fix handles the relevant failure modes.

## Observability Improvements

What metric, log, or alert would have shortened detection or diagnosis?

## Incident Communication

A concise stakeholder update, as if sending it to leadership and customer
support during the incident.
